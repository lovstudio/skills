# Cover Art Prompt Template

Create one high-resolution square editorial art master for a Chinese WeChat long-form article.

Article thesis: ARTICLE_THESIS
Core relationship: CORE_RELATIONSHIP
Primary visual metaphor: PRIMARY_VISUAL_METAPHOR
Material language: MATERIAL_LANGUAGE
Palette: PALETTE

Art direction: warm academic, humanist editorial illustration, paper-feel, matte print texture, elegant restraint, generous negative space, one unmistakable focal point. The image must support two separately composed assets: a 2.35:1 horizontal sharing cover and an independent 4:3 horizontal body opening image.

Composition constraints:

- Keep the central horizontal band calm and high-contrast for a later official Logo overlay.
- Keep the bottom 20 percent of the 4:3 body crop calm for a later bottom-centered Logo.
- Preserve the main metaphor in both center crops.
- No text, letters, numbers, pseudo-text, Logo, watermark, interface, code screen, robot, neon glow, generic particles, stock-photo desk scene, or decorative brand panel.
- Do not render the publication Logo. Branding is added later by a deterministic compositor.

Output only the unbranded art master.
