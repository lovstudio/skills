## 1. Truth ledger

Separate useful input before writing copy:

| Class | Meaning | Poster destination |
|---|---|---|
| Internal context | Planning notes, private anecdotes, competitor commentary | `brief.md` only |
| Publishable claim | Product-relevant statement supported by current truth | Visible copy |
| Verified proof | Real UI, artifact, case, metric, availability, source | Proof surface |
| Open evidence | Desired claim awaiting verification | Internal follow-up |

Current product behavior and approved brand truth outrank old copy. Generated
media changes presentation, never evidence level.

## 2. Brand Spine

Record one sentence for each:

- Category: familiar frame for what the product is.
- Audience tension: costly before-state or unresolved aspiration.
- Belief: consequential point of view.
- Promise: change the product accepts being measured against.
- Mechanism: product behavior that earns the promise.
- Difference: deliberate product choice visible in use.
- Personality: observable language and design behavior.
- Proof: strongest current reason to believe.
- Boundary: what the product deliberately avoids becoming.
- Action: real next state that completes the promise.

Compress these into one internal thesis. Public copy may express it indirectly.

## 3. One public thesis

A OnePage poster needs one memorable idea, one protagonist, and one action.
Choose the idea that best joins audience tension, product mechanism, and proof.
Supporting features explain the idea; they do not compete with it.

## 4. Headline spine

Read only the visible headings. They should form this movement:

```text
possibility or tension
→ product outcome
→ mechanism or transformation
→ proof or difference
→ concrete action
```

Use category labels for orientation, not as the story. Replace headings such as
“功能亮点” with the outcome or mechanism the section proves.

## 5. Proof and claims

Proof strength, strongest first:

1. direct working product experience;
2. real product screenshot, output, or demo;
3. attributable customer case;
4. sourced metric with definition and date;
5. specific mechanism explanation;
6. unsupported assertion.

Link visible proof to `data-source-ref="S1"`. Preserve units, period, source,
and caveat. Treat qualitative judgment as qualitative. Remove unsupported
superlatives, rankings, customer counts, awards, and causal claims.

## 6. Conversion

Choose one primary event: try, download, purchase, book, contact, join, or read.
CTA text names the next state, such as “开始创建”“下载 macOS 版”“预约演示”.
Its destination must exist or the copy must state availability clearly. A QR
code and printed URL must encode the same target.

## 7. Copy budgets

Count one CJK character or one Latin word as one semantic unit:

| Element | Normal | Hard ceiling |
|---|---:|---:|
| Product/category label | 2–10 | 16 |
| Main headline | 8–24 | 36 |
| Supporting line | 12–36 | 56 |
| Feature title | 2–10 | 16 |
| Feature explanation | 8–24 | 36 |
| Proof statement | 4–18 | 28 |
| CTA label | 2–8 | 12 |

Edit in this order: remove repetition, convert prose to precise labels, move
methodology to notes, remove a stage, then reduce type size.
