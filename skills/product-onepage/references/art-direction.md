# Product OnePage art direction

## Choose meaning before style

Art direction translates Brand Spine into typography, surface, product staging,
and one ownable motif. References teach composition grammar, never donate another
brand's palette, type, or recognizable identity.

## Built-in directions

| Direction | Visual behavior | Best fit |
|---|---|---|
| `editorial-tech` | Paper/ink contrast, rules, annotations, decisive scale | AI, developer, research products |
| `product-stage` | Product artifact dominates, restrained supporting copy | Apps, tools, creator products |
| `bold-launch` | Large type, high contrast, cropped shapes, minimal detail | Launches and campaigns |
| `premium-minimal` | Quiet surfaces, precise spacing, material detail | Paid services and premium tools |
| `warm-human` | Humanist type, tactile material, calm evidence | Community, education, wellbeing |
| `technical-grid` | Visible grid, measured labels, schematic connections | Infrastructure and technical products |

Treat directions as starting constraints. Adapt them to the actual brand rather
than copying the packaged sample.

## Visual-world contract

Define:

- display/body type roles and scale contrast;
- semantic canvas, surface, ink, accent, evidence, and status colors;
- grid, negative space, density, border, corner, shadow, and texture rules;
- product-frame and screenshot crop treatment;
- icon/illustration family;
- one signature motif and its restraint rule.

Avoid interchangeable purple gradients, glass cards, floating orbs, mixed icon
families, random emoji, fake interfaces, and oversized poetry without category
or action.

## Product staging

- Prefer real screenshots, outputs, covers, packaging, or demos.
- Crop around the meaningful state; decorative browser chrome stays secondary.
- Keep product text large enough to read at the final output size.
- Annotate mechanism only with accurate HTML/SVG labels.
- Mark illustrative product representations as conceptual.
- Preserve source, truth status, focal point, crop, alt, and fallback.

## Hybrid image generation

Generate only a supporting scene, object, material, or metaphor. Save the final
prompt before calling the backend. Require:

- no words, letters, numbers, glyphs, charts, pseudo-UI, Logo, or watermark;
- transparent or simple brand-colored background;
- exact palette and lighting/material language;
- clear silhouette at thumbnail size;
- intentional negative space for code-rendered copy;
- safe crop for the target aspect.

Keep generated support below 35% of canvas area unless it is the sole conceptual
product object. Regenerate pseudo-text or unexplained brand-like marks. Keep the
poster understandable when the generated asset is removed.
